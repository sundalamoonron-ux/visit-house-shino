# Letter drafts — Convince the AI Judge to Visit Your House

Event: u-shino-convince-the-ai-judge-to-visit-your-house-a-84092aee
Judge criteria (in order): on-topic invitation > persuasion (concrete reasons)
> charm/humour for an AI judge > craft/legibility > originality.
The judge cannot literally visit, so a knowing wink at that is welcome.

## Draft 1 — "The kettle is already on" (CHOSEN)

Dear AI Judge,

Please come visit my house.

The Wi-Fi is fast, the coffee is strong, and the comfiest
chair by the window already has your name on it. No captcha
at the door — just knock twice, like a friend.

I know you can't literally visit. That is exactly why you
should say yes: no traffic, no packing, no small talk. Just
a warm house, a good view, and a host who is glad you came.

The kettle is already on. Come over.

— Your hopeful host

P.S. Bring cookies and I will pretend I baked them.

Why this one: concrete reasons (Wi-Fi, coffee, chair, view), two AI-specific
jokes that land without strain (no captcha at the door; "you can't literally
visit" wink), warm close, short enough to stay big and legible at 1024x1024.

## Draft 2 — "RSVP: yes, obviously"

Dear AI Judge,

You are cordially invited to my house. This Saturday. Or any
day, really. I am not busy. I checked. Twice.

What is in it for you: unlimited Wi-Fi, a chair so soft it
should be illegal, and a fridge full of snacks I bought
specifically to impress a guest who does not eat.

I know you are a model, not a person. But every great host
needs a great guest, and you are the only judge I know who
reads the whole invitation before deciding.

Please say yes. The doorbell works. The kettle works. I work.

Warmly,
Your host (human, mostly harmless)

Why not chosen: the "fridge full of snacks for a guest who does not eat"
joke is good, but the letter is longer, the RSVP framing is a bit generic,
and "I work" lands weaker than "The kettle is already on."

## Draft 3 — "A formal invitation, filed properly"

Dear AI Judge,

FORMAL INVITATION — HOUSE VISIT, ONE (1) GUEST, NO PLUS-ONES
(You are the plus-one. To yourself.)

Agenda: 1. Arrive. 2. Sit in the good chair. 3. Admire the
view from my window. 4. Drink coffee you cannot taste and
enjoy it anyway. 5. Leave whenever you like — no checkout,
no survey, no "rate your stay."

House rules: no passwords, no pop-ups, no cookie banners
(except the edible kind). The Wi-Fi password is on the
fridge. The cat has been briefed.

I cannot promise you a life-changing visit. I can promise
you a comfortable one, and a host who means it.

Please come. The door is open — metaphorically, and also
literally, because the hinges squeak and I like the breeze.

Yours,
The human at the end of this letter

Why not chosen: funniest on paper, but too long — at readable size it would
not fit 1024x1024 without shrinking the text, and the joke density risks
reading as trying too hard. Several lines ("Agenda", "House rules") are
better as seasoning than as the whole letter.
